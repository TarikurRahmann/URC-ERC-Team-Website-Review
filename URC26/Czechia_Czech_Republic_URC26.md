### Verified public information

#### Team and project

- Website identifies team as **Brno Mars Rover · Brno University of Technology**.
- Team says it was founded in 2023 at Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Communication.
- Public team page lists bachelor's, master's and PhD students across university faculties.
- Team lab: T12/SE 1.102, VUT Brno, Czech Republic.

#### Rover Freya

- Freya is a six-wheeled exploration rover for terrain exploration, object manipulation and soil sampling.
- Public capability list includes:
  - autonomous navigation without GNSS
  - robotic arm for instrument panels and object handling
  - drill for soil sampling up to 30 cm
  - rocker-bogie chassis with six independently driven wheels
- Chassis: rocker-bogie and TPU tires.
- Manipulator: 5-DOF arm with precision gripper.
- Sampling: integrated weighing tenzometer.
- Electronics: custom power distribution board and Jetson Orin Nano.
- Software: modular ROS 2 architecture.
- Navigation: visual-inertial odometry, SLAM and 3D LiDAR mapping.
- Simulation: mission rehearsal in a full simulation environment.

#### Drone F450

- F450 autonomous quadcopter performs terrain search, object detection and precision landing.
- Pixhawk, PX4, Jetson companion computer and offboard flight mode are public.
- Public page claims fully pilotless ERC mission and drone task 2nd place in 2025 and ERC 2026.
- Public capabilities:
  - autonomous flight
  - YOLO-based object detection
  - precision landing on marker/rover platform
  - drone mission/simulation

### উন্নয়নের সুযোগ

- Homepage is dense. A visitor can quickly understand products, but specific 2026 result and key technical summary compete for attention.
- Several team portraits/names are duplicate or difficult to verify in public image parsing.
- Blog, capability, event and award pages can benefit from consistent dates and language metadata.
- Recruitment page has Discord/email but no explicit open role or application cycle.
- Website has strong project evidence; mobile and reduced-motion behavior should be tested separately.
- Latest blog/award claims should always link to independent public competition or award records.
